MPC MULTITRACK KIT: CREDITS AND LICENSES
========================================

The USB audio addin (MPC-USB-audio-addin-...zip)
  Original project: mpc-addin-usb-audio by Jacob Sabella
  https://github.com/jacob-sabella/mpc-addin-usb-audio
  Multitrack version (send channels for USB Out, 27 channels): soufprodev
  https://github.com/soufprodev/mpc-addin-usb-audio
  The installer scripts come from the Open MPC project (mpc-vst-plugins).

The USB Out plugin
